# Technical Article Generation Prompt

## Topic
[INSERT TOPIC]

## Author
Yogesh Sridhar

## Purpose
Create a technically mature learning article suitable for a postgraduate engineering portfolio.

## Writing Style
- Master’s-level / senior-engineer tone
- Concise and precise
- Minimal storytelling
- No filler
- Prefer mental models over textbook-style definitions
- Use practical examples
- Explain terminology clearly
- Avoid sounding like beginner study notes

## Required Structure
1. Title
2. Author and AI-assisted disclosure
3. Abstract
4. Clickable Table of Contents
5. Core mental model
6. Architecture / process diagram
7. Terminology table
8. Main concepts
9. Where each concept fits in the system or SDLC
10. Practical examples
11. Failure modes and edge cases
12. Testing / evaluation framework
13. Case study
14. One-page memory map
15. Key discussion points
16. Short conclusion

## Visual Style
- Sober and restrained
- Off-white background
- Charcoal text
- Muted grey / green accents
- No bright blue
- No gradients
- No neon colours
- No flashy cards
- Strong whitespace
- Narrow readable content width
- Professional tables
- Simple diagrams
- Technical-report / engineering-publication feel
- Print-friendly

## AI Disclosure
Use:

"AI-assisted for structuring, editing and presentation. The ideas, learning, examples, interpretations and conclusions are the author's own."

## Quality Check
Before finalising, review the article as if it were being reviewed by a senior software engineer.

Check:
- Is the technical hierarchy clear?
- Does it teach a mental model?
- Are examples realistic?
- Are tables concise?
- Are diagrams useful?
- Is anything repetitive?
- Does it feel postgraduate rather than beginner-level?
- Is the design restrained and professional?
