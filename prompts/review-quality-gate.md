# Technical Article Quality Gate

Use this checklist before publishing any article.

## Technical Accuracy

- Are technical terms used correctly?
- Are architecture diagrams accurate?
- Are examples realistic?
- Are claims clearly distinguished from opinions?
- Are time-sensitive claims verified?
- Are important trade-offs represented fairly?

## Structure

- Is there a clear title?
- Is there a short abstract?
- Is the Table of Contents clickable?
- Does the article progress logically?
- Are headings descriptive?
- Does each section add new information?

## Writing Quality

- Is the prose concise?
- Is there unnecessary repetition?
- Are paragraphs reasonably short?
- Are definitions followed by practical context?
- Does the writing sound technically mature?
- Is jargon explained where necessary?

## Visual Quality

- Is the colour palette restrained?
- Are tables readable?
- Are diagrams useful?
- Is whitespace sufficient?
- Is the page comfortable to read?
- Does it work on mobile?
- Is it print-friendly?

## Engineering Value

The article should answer:

- What is it?
- Why does it matter?
- How does it work?
- Where does it fit?
- How can it fail?
- How is it tested?
- What metrics matter?
- What trade-offs should engineers understand?

## Portfolio Safety

Before publishing:
- remove confidential information
- remove internal company screenshots
- remove API keys and secrets
- remove customer information
- remove proprietary prompts
- remove internal URLs
- avoid naming clients unless permitted

## AI Disclosure

Confirm that the article includes:

> AI-assisted for structuring, editing and presentation. The ideas, learning, examples, interpretations and conclusions are the author's own.

## Final Check

Would I be comfortable sending this article directly to:
- a senior engineer
- a hiring manager
- a technical recruiter
- a university supervisor

If not, revise before publishing.
